1. Who we are

Kuabo ("we", "us", "our") is an application and platform that guides Global Movers — migrants, expats, travelers, nomads — step by step after their visa. Kuabo is operated by its founder, currently based in Maryland, USA. Our website is kuabo.co.

This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you visit our website, join our waitlist, or use our app.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Waitlist & contact

  • Email address — when you sign up to our waitlist or contact us
  • Language preference — EN, FR, or ES
  • Country / region (optional) — to tailor your experience

2.2 App usage (post-launch)

  • Account data — username, password (hashed), profile info you provide
  • App activity — features used, badges earned, in-app actions (Akwɛ rewards, check-ins)
  • Device info — device type, OS version, language, timezone

2.3 Automatic

  • Analytics — aggregated, anonymized usage stats (via privacy-first tools)
  • Server logs — IP address (truncated), browser type, request times (for security)

3. How we use your data

  • To send you the launch announcement and product updates
  • To provide the Kuabo service (guides, AI chat, community)
  • To improve our product using anonymized analytics
  • To communicate with you about your account or important changes
  • To protect against fraud and ensure platform security
  • To comply with legal obligations

We never sell your data. Period.

4. Sharing & third parties

We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers required to deliver our service:

  • Vercel (website hosting, USA) — server logs only
  • Google Firebase (app backend, USA/EU) — account data, app activity
  • Resend (transactional email, USA) — your email when we send you updates
  • Stripe (post-launch payments, USA) — billing data if you subscribe to premium tiers
  • Plausible Analytics (EU-hosted) — anonymized website usage stats

All providers are bound by data processing agreements (DPAs) and meet GDPR standards.

5. Cookies & tracking

We use minimal cookies:

  • Essential cookies — language preference, session (no consent required)
  • Analytics cookies — Plausible Analytics is cookie-free and GDPR-compliant by design

We do not use Facebook Pixel, Google Ads tracking, or third-party advertising cookies.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectify — correct inaccurate data
  • Delete — ask us to erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — refuse certain processing (e.g., marketing)
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of waitlist or app at any time

To exercise any right, email support@kuabo.co. We respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

  • Waitlist email — kept until launch + 12 months, then deleted if no account created
  • Account data — kept while your account is active; deleted within 30 days of account closure
  • Audit logs — kept for 24 months for security compliance, then anonymized

8. Security

We take security seriously. We implement bank-grade measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption everywhere (TLS 1.3)
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt + salt
  • Two-factor authentication available
  • Rate limiting on sensitive endpoints
  • Anti-fraud scoring system
  • Real-time security monitoring
  • Comprehensive audit logging

If you discover a vulnerability, please report it responsibly to security@kuabo.co.

9. Children

Kuabo is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the minimum age required by your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Kuabo evolves. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notice. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

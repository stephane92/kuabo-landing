The story

Stéphane Adannou was born in Bénin 🇧🇯 and moved to the United States 🇺🇸. He lived what millions live: arriving somewhere new, facing paperwork, deadlines and a thousand unwritten rules — alone.

So he built the guide he wished he'd had. Kuabo turns settling into a new country into clear, gamified steps — documents, housing, jobs, community — in French, English and Spanish.

The mission

There are 281 million Global Movers on Earth — migrants, expats, travelers, nomads. They power the world, yet no product is built for their journey.

Kuabo is that product: the operating system for people who move. Built solo, from the ground up, for those who dare to move. 🌍🐦

Understanding visa types

Tourism, work, student, immigration — which visa for which project, and how to apply without getting scammed.

Comprendre les types de visa

Tourisme, travail, étudiant, immigration — quel visa pour quel projet, et comment faire sa demande sans se faire arnaquer.

Entender los tipos de visa

Turismo, trabajo, estudiante, inmigración — qué visa para qué proyecto y cómo solicitarla sin ser estafado.

Step by stepPas à pasPaso a paso
  1. Identify your goal: tourism (B1/B2), work (H1B, O1), study (F1), or permanent immigration (Green Card, DV).
  2. Go to the official embassy website of your target country and download the appropriate form.
  3. Prepare your documents: passport valid 6+ months, ID photos, proof of funds, motivation letter.
  4. Book a consulate appointment or submit your application online on the official portal.
  5. Wait for the decision (3 days to 6 months depending on type). Don't buy non-refundable tickets before getting the visa.

Pro tipsConseils de proConsejos pro
  • A tourist visa does NOT allow you to work — even voluntarily.
  • The Schengen visa covers 26 European countries with a single application.
  • Start your application 3 months before your planned travel date.
  • Always keep a digital copy of all your documents in the cloud.

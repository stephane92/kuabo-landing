Book your flight at the best price

Google Flights, Skyscanner, the cheapest days to fly, and how to avoid baggage surprises.

Réserver son vol au meilleur prix

Google Flights, Skyscanner, les jours les moins chers pour voler, et comment éviter les surprises de bagages.

Reservar tu vuelo al mejor precio

Google Flights, Skyscanner, los días más baratos para volar, y cómo evitar sorpresas de equipaje.

Step by stepPas à pasPaso a paso
  1. Use Google Flights: set price alerts for your destination and stay flexible on dates (+/- 3 days).
  2. Also compare on Skyscanner, Kayak and Kiwi.com — prices vary by platform.
  3. Tuesdays and Wednesdays are statistically the cheapest days to fly.
  4. Book 6-8 weeks ahead for Europe, 2-3 months for the USA/Asia.
  5. Consider flights with a layover — often 30-50% cheaper than direct.

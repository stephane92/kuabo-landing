Get a local SIM or eSIM

Stay connected from the moment you land — local SIM at the airport, eSIM before you leave, and no roaming surprises.

SIM locale ou eSIM dès l'arrivée

Reste connecté dès l'atterrissage — SIM locale à l'aéroport, eSIM avant de partir, et zéro surprise de roaming.

SIM local o eSIM al llegar

Mantente conectado desde que aterrizas — SIM local en el aeropuerto, eSIM antes de salir, y sin sorpresas de roaming.

Step by stepPas à pasPaso a paso
  1. At the airport, look for T-Mobile (USA), Orange (France), Airtel (Africa) counters depending on your destination.
  2. Buy a prepaid SIM with data — usually $15-30 for 30 days with 10-20GB.
  3. Alternatively, use an eSIM: buy online before departure on Airalo.com or Holafly.
  4. Set up the SIM right at the airport — no need to wait until you reach the city.
  5. Keep your original SIM for SMS and calls from your home country if needed.

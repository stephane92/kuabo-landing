Find safe temporary housing

Where to stay your first weeks, how to avoid the #1 scam targeting newcomers, and where to find long-term rentals.

Trouver un logement temporaire sûr

Où loger tes premières semaines, comment éviter l'arnaque n°1 des nouveaux arrivants, et où chercher du long terme.

Encontrar alojamiento temporal seguro

Dónde alojarte las primeras semanas, cómo evitar la estafa n°1 contra recién llegados, y dónde buscar alquileres largos.

Step by stepPas à pasPaso a paso
  1. Start with Airbnb or Booking.com for the first 2 weeks — comfort and safety guaranteed while you settle.
  2. Join local Facebook groups (e.g. "Housing NYC immigrants") to find shared housing and roommates.
  3. Use Craigslist (USA), SeLoger (France), Kijiji (Canada) for long-term listings.
  4. Visit in person before paying any deposit — never transfer money without a visit.
  5. Read the lease contract in full before signing — get it translated if needed.

Pro tipsConseils de proConsejos pro
  • Neighborhoods close to downtown are often too expensive — look 20-30 min away by transport.
  • Scam alert: if the rent is too low AND they ask for a transfer before any visit → it's a scam.
  • Facebook Marketplace is free and widely used for furniture and sublets.
  • Some cities have welcome centers for immigrants — ask at the town hall.
